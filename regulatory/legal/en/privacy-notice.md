## 2. Categories of users and data

### A. Personal users

If you use Agatha Track as a personal pet owner and not as part of an organisation, the data processed may include:
- account data, such as email address and login credentials;
- pet data, such as pet profiles, routines, medications, reminders, health or care logs, appointments, notes, and history you choose to record;
- collaboration data where you share a pet with another person;
- uploaded content, including photos of pets and photos or scans of documents;
- technical and security data, such as logs necessary to operate and secure the service.

Personal users who share a pet with another person do not receive broader personal profile access to that other person through the platform beyond what is needed for the shared pet functionality, as designed.

### B. Organisation users

If you use Agatha Track through an organisation, the data processed may include:
- account data, such as email address, login credentials, and name;
- optional profile data, such as phone number and address;
- role and permission data;
- foster-related operational data, including current and historical foster placements;
- organisation-scoped notes visible only to admins of the relevant organisation;
- uploaded content and technical/security data.

Phone number and address are optional based on the current configuration described by Agatha Track. Organisation-specific notes about registered users are scoped to the relevant organisation and are not intended to be visible across unrelated organisations.

### C. External fosters and other non-account contacts entered by organisations

An organisation may enter data about an external foster or another contact who does not have an Agatha Track account. That data may include:
- name;
- email address;
- phone number;
- address;
- organisation notes;
- fostered pets and related history.

Where such data is entered by an organisation, that organisation is responsible for having an appropriate lawful basis for doing so and for informing the individual as required by applicable data protection law. Agatha Track may send an information email to help explain the data held and the individual’s rights, but the organisation remains responsible for the underlying collection and use of that operational data.

## 3. Uploaded documents and images

Users may upload photos or documents, including pet photos and images of records or forms. Users are responsible for the content they upload and for ensuring that they have the right to upload it.

Agatha Track does not undertake to verify whether uploaded images or documents contain personal data, confidential information, or third-party information. Users should avoid uploading unnecessary personal data and should redact or remove information that is not needed. This is particularly important for scanned documents or photographs that may incidentally contain addresses, signatures, identity information, or other personal details.

## 4. Why data is processed and the legal bases

Agatha Track processes personal data for the following purposes:

### A. To create and manage accounts
This includes account registration, login, authentication, account security, and basic service administration. The legal basis is generally performance of a contract or steps taken at the user’s request before entering into a contract.

### B. To provide the core service
This includes pet management, reminders, care tracking, shared-pet features, collaboration, organisation workflows, foster administration, and related support functions. The legal basis is generally performance of a contract.

### C. To manage organisation-run foster operations
For organisation-managed directory data, foster records, operational notes, and external foster entries, the relevant organisation typically determines the purpose of the processing and is responsible for its own lawful basis, which may include legitimate interests or contractual/pre-contractual necessity depending on the context. Agatha Track processes such data on that organisation’s instructions where applicable.

### D. To communicate with users
This includes service messages, support responses, security notifications, and account-related communications. The legal basis is contract performance and, where relevant, legitimate interests in operating a secure and reliable service.

### E. To secure, maintain, and improve the platform
This includes troubleshooting, fraud prevention, abuse prevention, backups, system monitoring, and service reliability. The legal basis is legitimate interests in maintaining the safety, integrity, and performance of the service.

### F. To process payments
Where paid services are offered, payment-related processing is necessary for contract performance and associated billing administration.

### G. To comply with legal obligations
Certain processing may be necessary to meet legal, regulatory, tax, accounting, or law-enforcement obligations. The legal basis is compliance with a legal obligation.

Agatha Track does not use the data described in this notice for automated decision-making producing legal or similarly significant effects as described in Article 22 GDPR, based on the current service model.

If this changes, Agatha Track will update this notice and implement the appropriate legal safeguards required by applicable law.

## 7. How long data is kept

Agatha Track currently applies the following retention approach:
- account data: kept until the account is deleted, unless longer retention is required by law;
- backups: retained for up to 30 days;
- inactive accounts: may be deleted after 3 years of inactivity;
- external foster records: kept until deleted by the organisation, or after 5 years of inactivity following a check with the relevant organisation;
- some limited records may be retained longer where reasonably necessary for legal claims, dispute handling, security, fraud prevention, or compliance obligations.

Actual deletion may occur in stages, for example with short delays for backups, fraud prevention, or technical cleanup.
