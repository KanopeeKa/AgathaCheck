# Organization journeys

User-facing flows for charity/rescue organisation identity, discovery, and administration in AgathaTrack.

## Organisation profile and discovery

Public org profiles (`/o/orgs/:id`) present the organisation to visitors and members. Discovery flows help users find and join organisations.

Locked brief (IA partially superseded by v2): [organisation-dashboard-brief.md](organisation-dashboard-brief.md).

## People and permissions

Admins manage the member directory, roles, and staged permission changes (v4 people/permissions delivery plan).

## Member privacy (Account per-org)

Members control card, phone, email, and address visibility per organisation — see [org-member-privacy.md](org-member-privacy.md).

## Org pet inventory (identity view)

Org admins view org pet inventory, filters, and redacted pet cards. **Custody transfer workflows** (foster, adoption, return) are documented in the [fostering domain](/docs/domains/fostering/README.md).

## Branding and customisation

Organisation experience uses the teal theme and `logo-teal` assets (see specs). Org-specific colour rules are distinct from guardian plum — generic theming mechanism stays in [/docs/design/skin-change-guide.md](/docs/design/skin-change-guide.md).
